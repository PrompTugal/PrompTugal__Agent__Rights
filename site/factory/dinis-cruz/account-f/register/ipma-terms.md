## What follows for a promptugal.news page that charges nothing

- The terms allow use "for no purposes of profit". Dinis ruled (via the conductor, 26 Nov 16:30 UTC) that IPMA values may show on a promptugal.news page that carries no charge. The text on the page, "IPMA's terms, as the data site records them, allow its data to be used where no profit is made", is a fair short form of the clause above. Whether a free page on a site that bills elsewhere is "no purposes of profit" is a legal reading that is Dinis's, not mine; I record that he made it.
- Conditions to keep: IPMA named as the source on the page, next to the values; the link to IPMA's site; IPMA's logo where information is included (the terms say "should"); the visible "no fee is charged" line; no meter or "support us" prompt on that page.
- Other pages of promptugal.news that charge or meter stay without IPMA data (R1).
